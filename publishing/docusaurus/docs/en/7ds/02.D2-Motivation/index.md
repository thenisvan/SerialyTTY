# D2 - Motivation

## Why SerialyTTY?

Existing USB-to-Serial adapters are often "dumb" devices. They convert signals but offer no insight into the data, no standalone capabilities, and require a PC for everything.

**SerialyTTY changes that.**

We wanted a tool that:
- Can tell you the baud rate automatically.
- Can display data on a screen without a laptop.
- Can log data to an SD card for long-term debugging.
- Can be configured wirelessly via Bluetooth.

## The Drive
Our team is motivated by the desire to solve our own daily frustrations in embedded development. We are building the tool we wish we had.
