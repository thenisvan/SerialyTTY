# D1 - Management

This dimension covers the organizational aspects of the SerialyTTY project, including goals, strategies, and progress tracking.

## Overview

Effective management is crucial for the success of SerialyTTY. We utilize agile methodologies to adapt to new requirements while maintaining a clear roadmap.

### Key Areas
- **Goals & Expectations:** Defining what success looks like.
- **Planning:** Roadmaps and milestones.
- **Strategy:** How we approach development and release.
- **Reporting:** Keeping track of progress (led by Ondrej).

## Team Structure
The project is managed collaboratively, with distinct roles assigned to leverage individual strengths:
- **Denis Ivan:** Technical Lead & Implementation.
- **Danylo:** Architecture & Design.
- **Ondrej:** Management & Documentation.
